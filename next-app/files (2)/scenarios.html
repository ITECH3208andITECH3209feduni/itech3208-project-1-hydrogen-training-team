⚠ Ventilation System

Valves, regulators, tubing joints, and connectors on this bench are common leak points. Inspect fittings regularly for wear and damage.

⚠ Chemical Storage

Chemicals stored near hydrogen equipment pose a fire and explosion risk. Incompatible chemicals must be kept separated and labelled correctly.

⚠ Gas Cylinder Storage

Unsecured hydrogen cylinders are a serious hazard. Cylinders must be chained upright, stored away from heat sources, with valve caps in place.
