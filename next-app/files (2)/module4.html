⚠ Hazard 4

Chemical Storage

Read through all sections to complete this module. Then test your knowledge in the Quizzes.

01

Why Chemical Storage Matters

Proper chemical storage is important in any laboratory, especially in environments where hydrogen is present. Incorrectly stored chemicals can increase the risk of fire, explosion, toxic reactions, or equipment damage.

Hydrogen itself is highly flammable, which means it should be kept away from incompatible chemicals, heat sources, and ignition sources.

02

Common Chemical Storage Hazards

Unsafe chemical storage can include:

  • Storing flammable materials near ignition sources
  • Placing incompatible chemicals together
  • Poor labelling of containers
  • Leaking or damaged chemical containers
  • Overcrowded storage areas
  • Improper storage of gas cylinders
💡 These situations can increase the severity of accidents during a hydrogen leak or fire.
03

Safe Storage Practices

To maintain a safe laboratory environment:

  • Clearly label all chemicals and gas cylinders
  • Store flammable chemicals in approved storage areas
  • Keep incompatible chemicals separated
  • Secure hydrogen cylinders properly
  • Ensure storage areas are well ventilated
  • Regularly inspect containers for damage or leaks
💡 Storage areas should always remain clean, organised, and easy to access during emergencies.
04

Warning Signs of Unsafe Storage

Signs of poor chemical storage may include:

  • Damaged or leaking containers
  • Missing labels
  • Strong chemical odours
  • Blocked walkways or exits
  • Chemicals stored near heat or electrical equipment
  • Unsecured gas cylinders
💡 Any unsafe storage conditions should be reported immediately.
05

Emergency Response

If a chemical storage issue is identified:

  1. Avoid touching damaged containers unless trained
  2. Inform laboratory supervisors immediately
  3. Keep ignition sources away from the area
  4. Follow laboratory emergency procedures
  5. Evacuate if there is a major leak or fire risk

🔑 Key Takeaway

Proper chemical storage is essential for maintaining safety in hydrogen laboratories. Organised storage, correct labelling, good ventilation, and regular inspections help reduce the risk of leaks, fires, and hazardous reactions.
